How to Create a User

If you don't have superuser access:

  1. Contact the person who has superuser access. Alternatively, contact the product owner Frank A at frank.a@unimelb.edu.au
  2. Ask them to create an account for you.

If you do have superuser access:

  1. Log into the user creation database, with super user credentials and click on Users.
  2. Click on Add User.
  3. Choose a username and password.
  4. Click Save.
  5. Go back to the main website.
